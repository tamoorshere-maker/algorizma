II The Lost Record

Masterpieces do not vanish. Their records do.

Some were cut from their frames. Others were simply forgotten: misattributed, inherited, hung in a hallway for a century. None of them ceased to exist. The trail is there. It is simply too vast, too scattered and too multilingual for any human team to read.

Today, finding them depends on a handful of specialists and a great deal of luck. Cases stay open for decades. Masterpieces sell for the price of decoration.

III Method

Four disciplines. One continuous trail.

What once took a career of expertise and a lifetime of luck, Algorizma compresses into a single, repeatable investigation.

  1. I

    The Dossier

    Every search begins with what is known: images, dimensions, provenance and the last confirmed sighting, assembled into one complete record of the missing work.

  2. II

    The Wide Reading

    The engine reads across publicly available sources, in many languages and at a scale no human team could match.

  3. III

    The Resemblance

    Proprietary visual analysis surfaces candidates that resemble the work, even when cropped, reframed, restored or poorly photographed.

  4. IV

    The Lead File

    Every candidate is ranked, documented and reviewed by a person. Leads go to rightful owners, insurers or the proper authorities, never to the public.

V Why Privately

Stolen works move when attention turns toward them.

A public launch would alert the very people holding them, and invite careless imitators into a field where one clumsy search can drive a masterpiece underground for another generation.

In the search for forgotten masterpieces, the advantage belongs to whoever sees first. That advantage disappears the moment the method becomes common knowledge.

So before anything is announced, we are speaking with a small circle of patrons and investors, privately, by invitation.

  • i

    Lawful channels

    Leads go to rightful owners, insurers and the proper authorities. We never act on a work ourselves.

  • ii

    Human judgement

    Every lead is reviewed by a person before it leaves the house. The engine suggests; people decide.

  • iii

    Discretion

    Cases, sources and findings are never published. Each engagement is reviewed against the laws of every country involved.
